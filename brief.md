This is a project that educates the public about the social model of disability. It has 3 elements :

1. Create your character - these are robots that you can specify the size and shape and certain characteristics such as speed, forgetfulness, balance, vision etc. There should be a broad range of options to make your robot unique and interesting
2. Put the character in the city - the robot autonomously follows the road below it as it tries to work its way around the city to pick up. The game for the user is to modify the city so that the robot can make the most use of it. There should be hazards and traps in the city that make it hard to navigate. There should be power-ups and things to collect that also add to the data set from the robot. The main interface is using touch to move and resize as well as add new elements to the environment. The issues that affect humans in the real world in terms of accessibility should also affect the robots in the game - mimicking human reality.
3. The data is made into music and art in real time that is unique to that robot and that route in the city, and that helps illustrate the plight and journey of the robot, using the language of music to communicate emotion.

The story goes that you have invested in an artbot (a robot that lives to make art with humans) and that you want to fix them up and put them to work, but you realise that the city is not designed for robots and so you make that your job - making the city accessible and inclusive. 

Research this and write up documentation. The tech stack should be using Babylon JS with an Orthogonal camera looking directly at the robot rotating in part 1 but looking down on the city in part 2. part 3 is an overlaid HUD. Add that we want the art and music to be saveable by the end user.
