    Firmware & setup
    1. Download Robot Comms firmware (.hex). The deployed site includes firmware built automatically by GitHub Actions.
    2. Connect your micro:bit V2 by USB, then copy robot-comms.hex to the MICROBIT drive. Wait for flashing to finish and the LED message pattern to appear.
    3. Leave the board powered; close other Bluetooth apps connected to it. Choose Connect micro:bit above and select your BBC micro:bit.
    4. Press B to test reception, then send a command here to test the LEDs and acknowledgement.

    This test firmware allows nearby devices to connect without pairing. A disconnect stops the demo; reconnection requires another click. See the firmware guide and protocol. If the download is unavailable on a local preview, get the robot-comms-firmware artifact from a successful firmware build and unzip it.
