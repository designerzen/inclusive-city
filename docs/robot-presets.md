# Robot presets

Select **Choose existing** in the editor to open the preset carousel. One robot appears centrally; the side arrows (or keyboard left/right arrows) slide through all five presets and wrap around. **Edit robot** takes the displayed robot into the editor. Each has a distinctive wheeled body, five paired ability allocations, exactly three enabled functions, and a creative preference. Reduced-motion preferences disable the sliding animation.

| Robot | Ability emphasis | Creative personality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MonetBot | Fine visual detail, patient movement, long-lasting energy | Impressionistic colour dabs and spacious ambient music |
| donkBot | Fast travel, agility and burst power | Driving techno with a four-beat kick and offbeat bass |
| HokusaiBot | Long reach, steady movement and detailed vision | Fine ink gestures and ambient music |
| CalderBot | Agility and compactness | Geometric shapes and swung jazz phrases |
| AriaBot | Route memory and endurance | Ringing melodic chimes |

Presets are starting points: rename them and adjust their settings in the normal editor. Selecting a preset again restores its cached instance, including edits and journey records. Previous/next navigation includes selected presets alongside random designs. Newly randomised bodies retain the current creative preference.

Creative preferences are saved in robot metadata and journey snapshots. Paint strokes carry their style so live rendering and exported paintings agree. Musical phrases remain JSON-safe scores suitable for playback and export. Environmental events still influence the work: barriers change the mood, access improvements bring relief, and pickups expand the palette and harmony.
